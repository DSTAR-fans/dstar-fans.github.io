Hamvention D-STAR Information

D-STAR related Hamvention info

Field Day 2025

ARRL Field Day is an annual event that takes place the 4th full weekend in June. The 2025 event will be held on January 28th-29th, 2025.

This is a great opportunity to get out and test your gear in the cold weather, while running D-STAR.

Winter Field Day 2026

Winter Field Day is an annual event that takes place the last full weekend in January. The 2026 event will be held on January 24th-25th, 2026.

This is a great opportunity to get out and test your gear in the cold weather, while running D-STAR.

D-STAR QSO PARTY 2025

The D-STAR QSO party is back, over the holiday period from December 27th, 2025 00:00 UTC to January 5th, 2026 23:59 UTC.

The purpose is to encourage D-STAR operators to communicate with as many other operators worldwide but also to encourage the picture-sending function found in the latest D-STAR radios.

Log submission deadline: January 21st, 2026 23:59 UTC

Results will be announced on: February 28th, 2026

December 27th, 2025 00:00 UTC - January 5th, 2026 23:59 UTC
